# Student guide

1. **Create an account** — *Join free* (`/signup`): email, password, confirm you are 18+ and accept the terms. Open the verification link from your email (in development: `/dev/outbox`).
2. **Find a teacher** — *Find Teachers* (`/teachers`): search by subject or keyword and filter by level, language, price, online/in-person, rating, city and radius. Switch to **Map** to see approximate teacher areas (never home addresses). "Use my location" is optional; you can pick a city instead.
3. **Open a profile** — read the bio, subjects, credentials status, offerings, reviews and cancellation policy. Use the heart to save a teacher (*Saved*).
4. **Book** — choose an offering, duration and an available slot (shown in your timezone). The slot is held for a few minutes while you check out. Free/trial lessons are confirmed immediately. Paid lessons need the online payment provider; if it is not configured you will see a clear notice and nothing is charged.
5. **Your lessons** — *My Lessons* (`/lessons`) lists upcoming and past lessons. Download a calendar file (.ics), cancel (the refund follows the policy shown before booking), or message the teacher.
6. **Join the class** — *Join lesson* becomes available 10 minutes before the start and until 15 minutes after the end, for online lessons. Your browser asks for camera/microphone; recording is off.
7. **After the lesson** — read the teacher's notes and leave one review for a completed lesson you attended.
8. **Courses** — browse *Courses*, enrol, work through lessons and quizzes in *My Courses*. A certificate with a public verification link is issued only when all lessons are done and the quiz is passed.
9. **Account** — *Settings*: language (English/Français/العربية), timezone, password, sign out of all devices, request a data export or account deletion. *Payments* lists your orders.
